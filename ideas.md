## Selected Design: Idea 1 — Analog Film / Western Americana

This design philosophy is the most authentic expression of the Moots Vibe Marketing concept. The lo-fi, grainy film aesthetic directly mirrors the campaign's creative direction ("no studio lighting — just a dusty Moots Routt leaning against a weathered fence"). The typewriter monospace reinforces the "field notes" quality of the captions. The earth-tone palette maps directly to the three territories: Ozark flint, Texas dust, Oklahoma prairie.
