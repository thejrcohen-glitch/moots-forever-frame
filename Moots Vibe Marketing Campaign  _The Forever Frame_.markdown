# Moots Vibe Marketing Campaign: "The Forever Frame"

## 1. Campaign Concept: "Arkansas Dust & Post-Ride Lonestar"

**The Vibe:** 
This campaign shifts the focus away from technical specifications—such as stiffness-to-weight ratios or aerodynamic drag—and instead sells the *feeling* of the ride. It is about the endurance of titanium, the grit of the trail, and the reward at the end. The aesthetic is lo-fi, grainy film, and authentic. It’s a dusty Moots Routt leaning against a weathered fence at a trailhead, not a studio-lit product shot.

**The Message:** 
"No carbon expiration dates. Just the sound of the tires on the flint hills and the promise of a cold one at the finish. Built in Colorado, proven in the Ozarks."

## 3. "Pop-up Espresso & Dirt" Event Guide

**The Strategy:** 
Partner with the coolest independent coffee roasters and craft breweries located near major gravel and cycling trails in Texas, Oklahoma, and Arkansas. The Moots rep brings a demo fleet to these locations for a morning "Espresso & Dirt" ride, followed by an afternoon "Pints & Pedals" session.

**Ideal Conditions:** 
Target weekends with clear skies and temperatures around 72°F (typically mid-to-late October or early April in this region).

### Target Locations & Partners

#### Bentonville, Arkansas (The Ozarks Vibe)
*The epicenter of modern trail riding, blending deep woods with high-end coffee culture.*

| Partner Type | Name | Location | The Vibe |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Coffee** | **Airship Coffee at Coler** | 1300 Applegate Trail, Bentonville, AR | An open-air, concrete-arch cafe located *inside* the Coler Mountain Bike Preserve. No front door, no Wi-Fi, just swings, coffee, and trail access [1]. The ultimate "if you know, you know" spot. |
| **Brewery** | **Bike Rack Brewing Co.** | 801 SE 8th St, Bentonville, AR | Bentonville's go-to brewery for trail-ready craft beer, deeply embedded in the local cycling community [2]. |

## 4. Execution Checklist for the Sales Rep

1.  **Advance Scouting:** Contact the owners of Airship (Bentonville), Flat Track (Austin), and Elemental (OKC) 4-6 weeks in advance to propose the "Pop-up Espresso & Dirt" morning.
2.  **Demo Fleet Prep:** Ensure the demo van is stocked with Routt 45s and Vamoots RSLs in a full size run.
3.  **Local Amplification:** Have the partner coffee shops tease the event on their Instagram stories using the lo-fi aesthetic.
4.  **The Event:** Set up the Moots tent *subtly*. No massive banners. Just the bikes, good espresso, and conversations about titanium welds and tire clearance.
5.  **The Handoff:** Direct interested riders to the local premium Moots dealer (e.g., Mellow Johnny's in Austin, Phat Tire in Bentonville) to finalize their custom builds.
